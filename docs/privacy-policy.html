3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App requests the following explicit device permissions on Android:

  • CAMERA (android.permission.CAMERA): Allows you to photograph animals directly inside the app to identify species and save them to your collection. The camera is never accessed in the background.
  • LOCATION (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used strictly on-demand when saving an animal discovery card to record the geographical coordinates where the animal was spotted. You can decline this permission at any time and still continue using the app.
  • READ / WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE: Used exclusively to temporarily store captured animal photos and export shareable discovery cards to your device storage.
  • INTERNET: Required to authenticate with Google, upload your animal observations to your secure cloud album, and query the AI species identification service.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Species Analysis & Data Privacy

Animal Collection offers an AI-powered species identification feature powered by the Google Gemini API.

  • Image Sanitization: Before transmitting any photograph to the AI service, the image is automatically resized and all EXIF metadata (including hardware serials and embedded GPS coordinates) is stripped to safeguard your privacy.
  • Scope of Processing: Photos sent to the AI service are analyzed exclusively to identify the animal species, breed, taxonomy, and dietary habits.
  • No Personal Identification: The AI model is instructed not to retain or process facial recognition or personal identifying information.
  • User Consent: The app requests explicit consent prior to sending images for AI analysis.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers essential for operating the App:

  • Google Identity Services (Google LLC): Facilitates secure user authentication via Google Sign-In. (Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google Generative AI / Gemini API (Google LLC): Performs image classification and species identification for uploaded animal photos.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database: User profiles and synced collections are securely stored using enterprise-grade cloud hosting and PostgreSQL/S3-compatible storage with encryption at rest and in transit.

6. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All network transmissions between the App and backend servers are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
  • User authentication sessions are handled via cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Sensitive session credentials stored on your device are protected using secure system keychains (SecureStore).

User Account & Data Deletion Instructions

At Animal Collection, we believe you should always maintain full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account and all data directly inside the mobile app in real-time:

  1. Open the Animal Collection app on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Settings screen (gear icon in your profile).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation dialog to confirm your intent.
  5. Tap Delete My Account.

Upon confirmation, your profile, authentication records, saved animal collections, notes, and uploaded photos will be immediately and irreversibly purged from our active databases and cloud storage.

Method 2: Deletion Request via Email / Web

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team:

Submit a Data Deletion Request

Send an email from the Google account associated with your Animal Collection profile to:

Email: hattakorn49@gmail.com

Subject: Animal Collection - Account & Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • The email address linked to your Google Sign-In account in the App.
  • A brief statement requesting the deletion of your account and data.

Processing Time: All deletion requests received via email are verified and processed within 7 business days (and in all cases within the 30-day statutory window). We will send a confirmation email once your data has been completely erased.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your Google profile details (Name, Email address, Avatar URL, and Google ID).
  • All animal discovery cards, photos, custom names, stories, and timestamps.
  • All geographic location coordinates associated with your discoveries.
  • Your badge progress, statistics, and favorite marks.

What Data is Retained?

No personal or identifying data is retained following account deletion. Any temporary server access logs required for security and fraud prevention are automatically deleted after 30 days.
